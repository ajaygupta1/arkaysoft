Our story

A partner for mission-critical software

Arkay Software Solutions Limited was founded on a simple conviction: institutions that carry public trust deserve software engineered to the highest standard of security, reliability and compliance.

For over 15 years we have partnered with banks, regulators, insurers and government agencies to design, build and modernise the systems their operations depend on. Today our work spans bespoke development, strategic consulting and our own family of enterprise software products.

We remain independent, engineering-led and deeply invested in the long-term success of every client we serve.
